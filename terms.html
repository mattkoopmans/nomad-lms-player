What Nomad is

Nomad is an Android app that turns your phone into a player for your own Lyrion Music Server (LMS). It plays the music, podcasts and radio your server offers, keeps copies of music you choose for playing without a connection, and can control playback. Nomad has no servers of its own, no accounts and no content: everything it plays comes from your Lyrion server, from the services your server is signed in to, or from podcast publishers directly.
